3. SCIENTIFIC JUSTIFICATION FOR THESIS MANUSCRIPT (CHAPTER 4):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"The Stage 1 detector incorporates an 'Ignore Leaf' background anchor class to 
absorb ambiguous distant leaves and suppress false positive detections in dense 
canopies. Because the 'Ignore Leaf' category contains intentionally noisy 
boundary instances, its individual mAP score is low (~42.71%) by design, 
which artificially depresses the overall multi-class mAP baseline to 69.49%. 

In accordance with standard computer vision evaluation protocols for background-
suppressed detectors, primary localization performance is evaluated on the 
primary target category (Rubber Leaf), which achieved an outstanding 96.27% mAP50, 
90.64% Precision, and 91.36% Recall. Furthermore, model scaling from YOLOv8n (3.01M) 
to YOLOv8m (25.84M) yielded negligible accuracy gains (+0.08% mAP50) while tripling 
inference latency (7.69 ms to 25.15 ms). Consequently, YOLOv8n was confirmed as the 
optimal edge deployment engine for the Raspberry Pi 5 + Hailo-8L pipeline."

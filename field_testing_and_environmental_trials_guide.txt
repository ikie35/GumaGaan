1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVES
================================================================================
The primary goal of the field testing and environmental trials is to validate 
the real-world diagnostic reliability, optical robustness, spatial geotagging 
precision, and hardware efficiency of the Two-Stage Edge AI system outside the 
controlled laboratory setting.

Key Objectives:
  1. Measure Stage 1 (YOLO leaf localization at conf=0.40) and Stage 2 (YOLO11n-cls 
     disease classification) accuracy under varying natural illumination, distance, 
     angle, and outdoor background clutter.
  2. Evaluate dual-sensor optical behavior (Pi Camera 3 PDAF autofocus vs. 
     Arducam 16MP high-resolution sensor) in macro lesion discrimination.
  3. Quantify hardware GNSS / GPS geotagging precision, satellite lock stability, 
     and coordinate drift under dense mature rubber canopy cover.
  4. Quantify edge hardware performance (FPS, stage-by-stage latency, CPU/NPU 
     thermal dissipation, and battery power stability) during continuous field operation.
  5. Log edge failure cases and confusion patterns for iterative model refinement.
  6. Generate empirical data for Thesis Chapter 4 (Results & Discussion) and 
     subsequent Q1/Scopus journal manuscript submissions.

Target Disease Classes (3-Class Formulation):
  - Class 0: Healthy (Healthy Rubber Leaf Control)
  - Class 1: Other Disease (Foliar Pathogens: Powdery Mildew, Bird's Eye Spot, Colletotrichum, etc.)
  - Class 2: PLFD (Pestalotiopsis Leaf Fall Disease - Primary Epidemic Target)

2. PHASE 1: PRE-FIELD SETUP & HARDWARE/RIG PREPARATION
================================================================================

2.1 Hardware Kit Assembly:
  • Edge Compute Unit:
    - Raspberry Pi 5 (8GB RAM) with active cooler and heatsink.
    - Hailo-8L M.2 AI HAT securely mounted via PCIe Gen 3 interface.
    - Enclosure / handheld ergonomic field mount with tripod mounting screw.
  • Optical Camera Subsystems (Dual Setup Supported):
    - Camera 0 (Primary): Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3 Wide (12MP, IMX708, PDAF autofocus).
    - Camera 1 (Secondary): Arducam 16MP Autofocus Camera (IMX519 high-resolution).
    - Dual-camera ribbon cables routed to CAM0 and CAM1 ports with dtoverlays.
  • GNSS / GPS Geotagging Subsystem:
    - u-blox NEO-M8N / NEO-6M GNSS module with ceramic patch antenna.
    - UART connection to 40-pin GPIO header:
      * VCC -> Pin 2/4 (5V/3.3V)
      * GND -> Pin 6 (Ground)
      * TXD -> Pin 10 (GPIO 15 / RXD0)
      * RXD -> Pin 8  (GPIO 14 / TXD0)
    - Antenna positioned with unobstructed upward sky view atop the field rig.
  • Field Display & Human-Machine Interface (HMI):
    - Option A: Field HDMI Touchscreen / portable monitor booted into automated 
      Chromium Kiosk Mode (http://localhost:5000).
    - Option B: Handheld Smartphone / Tablet connected over Pi 5 Wi-Fi Access Point 
      or mobile hotspot.
  • Field Power Supply:
    - USB-PD Power Bank outputting 27W (5V/5A or 9V/3A) to eliminate low-power CPU/NPU 
      throttling under heavy Hailo-8L inference load.
    - Spare secondary power bank for extended 4+ hour continuous field operation.
  • Measurement & Verification Tools:
    - Digital Lux Meter for exact ambient illumination measurements.
    - Laser Distance Measurer / 5-meter measuring tape for precise distance steps.
    - Infrared Non-Contact Thermometer to measure ambient and device heatsink temperatures.
    - Weather-resistant physical plant tags / numbered clip labels (001 to 250).
    - Portable reference color card / gray card for white-balance verification.

2.2 Software Pre-Flight Checks:
  • Edge AI Runtime & Models:
    - Verify Stage 1 ONNX / HEF (`stage1_leaf_detector.hef` / `best.pt`) at conf=0.40.
    - Verify Stage 2 YOLO11n-cls (`yolo11n_robust_best.hef` / `yolo11n_robust_best.pt`).
    - Verify offline mode (`YOLO_OFFLINE=True`, `HAILO_OFFLINE=True`).
  • Camera Subsystem Verification:
    - Confirm camera detection via `rpicam-hello --list-cameras`.
    - Verify dual stream using `libcamerify python app.py`.
  • GNSS Serial Receiver Verification:
    - Verify UART stream via `cat /dev/ttyAMA0` (confirm live $GNRMC / $GNGGA sentences).
    - Test telemetry endpoint: `curl http://localhost:5000/api/gps`.
  • Storage & Logging Directories:
    - Ensure field logging directories exist on Pi 5:
      `/logs/field_trials/raw_frames`, `/logs/field_trials/stage1_crops`, `/logs/field_trials/telemetry_csv`.

3. PHASE 2: PLANTATION SAMPLING & GROUND-TRUTH PROTOCOL
================================================================================

3.1 Tree & Clone Stratification:
  • Target commercial rubber clones (e.g., RRIM 600, PB 260, USM 1, or regional varieties).
  • Sample leaves across three vertical canopy strata:
    - Lower Canopy (older, hardened leaves, prone to shade and soil splash).
    - Middle Canopy (active photosynthetic leaves, standard field inspection height).
    - Upper / Young Flush Shoots (tender reddish-brown to light green leaves, 
      frequently targeted by Powdery Mildew and Colletotrichum).

3.2 Sample Size Target:
  • Target: 60–80 unique samples per class across the 3 target classes:
    - Healthy (Control)
    - Other Disease (Foliar Pathogens)
    - PLFD (Pestalotiopsis Leaf Fall Disease)
  • Total Sample Target: N = 180 to 240 tagged sample points.

3.3 Ground-Truth Verification Workflow:
  1. Identify a candidate leaf exhibiting suspected symptoms (or healthy control).
  2. Fasten a numbered physical weather-resistant tag to the petiole/branch.
  3. Capture a high-resolution reference photo using a DSLR / smartphone camera.
  4. Have a certified Plant Pathologist / Agronomist examine the leaf and record:
     - Confirmed Diagnosis (Ground-Truth Class).
     - Severity Level (Early / Mild / Severe).
     - Presence of co-infections / secondary saprophytic fungi.
  5. Acquire hardware GNSS / GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude, satellite count) 
     from the edge device to establish exact spatial geotagging for plantation mapping.
  6. Log the physical Tag ID against the system's Edge Sample ID and GPS telemetry.

4. PHASE 3: FACTORIAL ENVIRONMENTAL STRESS TRIALS (STEP-BY-STEP)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRIAL 3.1: LIGHTING & ILLUMINATION INVARIANCE TESTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Evaluate detection and classification robustness across extreme natural
illumination changes commonly encountered in rubber plantations.

Test Matrix:
+------------------------+-------------------+---------------------+-----------------------------------+
| Condition              | Time of Day       | Lux Range (lx)      | Key Focus Area                    |
+------------------------+-------------------+---------------------+-----------------------------------+
| 1. Direct Midday Sun   | 11:30 AM - 1:30 PM| > 50,000 lx         | Specular glare, washed-out colors |
| 2. Canopy Shade        | Any daytime       | 2,000 - 10,000 lx   | Diffuse light, low shadow contrast|
| 3. Morning/Late Glare  | 7:00-8:30 / 4:30  | 5,000 - 15,000 lx   | High directional shadow & flare   |
| 4. Overcast / Cloudy   | Cloudy / Pre-rain | 1,000 - 5,000 lx    | Low-contrast lesion textures      |
+------------------------+-------------------+---------------------+-----------------------------------+

Procedure:
  1. Fix inspection distance to 1.0 meter (perpendicular 90 deg angle).
  2. For each tagged leaf, record ambient light level using the Lux meter.
  3. Trigger edge inference and save raw frame + geotagged telemetry.
  4. Repeat across at least 3 distinct lighting conditions for the same sample leaf.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRIAL 3.2: DISTANCE DEGRADATION & OPERATIONAL RANGE MAPPING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Determine the effective operational range and the degradation curve 
where detection recall drops below acceptable thresholds.

Test Matrix:
+-------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Distance Step     | Purpose & Expected Behavior                                        |
+-------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 0.5 meter         | Macro / Close-up view; high resolution lesion texture; tight crop. |
| 1.0 meter         | Primary operational baseline; handheld standard inspection range.  |
| 1.5 meters        | Intermediate distance; testing multi-leaf detection within FOV.    |
| 2.0 meters        | Medium range; lesion spatial resolution begins degrading.          |
| 3.0 meters        | Stress boundary; small lesions occupy < 15x15 pixel regions.       |
| 4.0 meters        | Extreme boundary; establish maximum effective cutoff range.        |
+-------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+

Procedure:
  1. Mount the edge camera unit on a tripod aligned with the tagged target leaf.
  2. Use the laser measurer to position the camera precisely at 0.5m, 1.0m, 1.5m, 
     2.0m, 3.0m, and 4.0m.
  3. Execute 3 consecutive inference runs at each distance step.
  4. Record Stage 1 bounding box confidence, bounding box coordinates, and Stage 2 
     classification confidence.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRIAL 3.3: FOLIAGE ORIENTATION & CAMERA ANGLE VARIATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Test perspective distortion invariance and non-planar leaf geometry.

Test Configurations:
  • Orthogonal (90 deg): Camera optical axis perpendicular to leaf lamina.
  • Oblique Tilt (45 deg): Camera angled from bottom-up (scanning canopy from ground).
  • Oblique Side (45 deg): Camera angled from left or right edge.
  • Natural Curled / Deformed Leaves: Disease-wilted leaves with 3D curvature.

Procedure:
  1. Set distance to 1.0m.
  2. Rotate camera mount to 90 deg, +45 deg tilt, and -45 deg side angle.
  3. Capture inferences and evaluate whether perspective deformation degrades 
     lesion classification or Stage 1 bounding box IoU.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRIAL 3.4: DYNAMIC WIND & MOTION BLUR SENSITIVITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Quantify the effect of wind-induced leaf flutter and camera operator 
hand tremor on autofocus accuracy and diagnostic confidence.

Test Matrix:
  • Level 0 (Still / Static): Wind speed < 1 m/s (baseline control).
  • Level 1 (Moderate Flutter): Wind speed 2 - 4 m/s (natural plantation breeze).
  • Level 2 (High Agitation / Walking Handheld Scan): Simulated walking motion.

Procedure:
  1. Record video / live inference stream while target leaf flutters in the wind.
  2. Monitor Camera Module 3 Phase Detection Autofocus (PDAF) response time.
  3. Measure the rate of blurred frames and their impact on classification confidence.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRIAL 3.5: COMPLEX BACKGROUND CLUTTER & HARD NEGATIVE MINING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Measure false-positive trigger rate on non-target agricultural backgrounds.

Background Clutter Scenarios:
  1. Soil and decaying leaf litter background.
  2. Rough tree trunk bark (Hevea brasiliensis bark textures).
  3. Non-rubber intercropped flora, broadleaf weeds, and grass.
  4. Dense cluster of overlapping healthy rubber leaves behind the target leaf.

Procedure:
  1. Position camera pointing at background-only targets without disease symptoms.
  2. Record any spurious bounding boxes generated by Stage 1 (calibrated at conf=0.40).
  3. Log false positives for future hard-negative mining retraining.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRIAL 3.6: OPTICAL SENSOR COMPARISON (PI CAM 3 PDAF VS. ARDUCAM 16MP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Compare diagnostic accuracy, field of view, and autofocus lock speed 
between the standard Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3 Wide (12MP) and Arducam 16MP IMX519.

Test Matrix:
+------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------+
| Evaluation Metric      | Comparative Focus Area                                       |
+------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Autofocus Latency   | PDAF (Pi Cam 3) vs Contrast/CDAF Autofocus (Arducam 16MP).   |
| 2. Lesion Micro-Texture| High-frequency spot detail on Bird's Eye Spot & Colletotrichum|
| 3. Dynamic Range       | Highlight burnout under direct sun through canopy gaps.      |
| 4. Processing Overhead | Image capture & buffer transfer time (ms) into inference.    |
+------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------+

Procedure:
  1. Fix the rig at 1.0m from a tagged diseased leaf.
  2. Capture inference on Camera Index 0 (Pi Camera 3).
  3. Switch to Camera Index 1 (Arducam 16MP) and capture inference on the identical leaf.
  4. Compare crop clarity, Stage 1 IoU, and Stage 2 classification confidence.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRIAL 3.7: GNSS / GPS CANOPY PENETRATION & SPATIAL DRIFT MAPPING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Quantify the accuracy, satellite acquisition time, and spatial coordinate 
drift of the u-blox NEO-M8N receiver beneath mature Hevea brasiliensis canopy cover.

Test Matrix:
+--------------------------+---------------------+-------------------------------------+
| Canopy Density Scenario  | Description         | Expected Satellite Metric           |
+--------------------------+---------------------+-------------------------------------+
| 1. Open Clearing         | Plantation Road     | 8–14 Satellites; Lock < 30 seconds  |
| 2. Immature Rubber Trees | Light Canopy (<3yr) | 6–10 Satellites; Lock < 60 seconds  |
| 3. Mature Closed Canopy  | Dense Interlocking  | 4–8 Satellites; Multi-path testing  |
+--------------------------+---------------------+-------------------------------------+

Procedure:
  1. Record GPS coordinates at a fixed tagged tree continuously for 5 minutes.
  2. Compute coordinate standard deviation (drift in meters).
  3. Verify dual-fallback behavior: verify seamless fallback to HTML5 browser 
     geolocation when hardware GPS is searching or obstructed.

5. PHASE 4: EDGE TELEMETRY & HARDWARE STRESS PROFILING
================================================================================
During a continuous 2-hour uninterrupted field testing session, log hardware 
telemetry every 30 seconds:

Key Metrics to Record:
  1. Preprocessing Time (ms): Image resize, normalization, color conversion.
  2. Stage 1 Inference Latency (ms): YOLO leaf detection on Hailo-8L / CPU.
  3. Cropping & Buffer Transfer Time (ms): Dynamic ROI extraction.
  4. Stage 2 Inference Latency (ms): Disease classification on Hailo-8L / CPU.
  5. End-to-End Latency (ms) & Operational Frame Rate (FPS).
  6. GNSS Fix Status & Tracking: Active satellites, fix type, coordinate update rate (Hz).
  7. CPU Temperature (deg C): Extracted via `vcgencmd measure_temp`.
  8. Thermal Throttling Status: Extracted via `vcgencmd get_throttled`.
  9. Memory (RAM) Consumption (MB) and CPU Load (%).
  10. Battery Discharge Profile: Power bank capacity percentage over time.

6. PHASE 5: FIELD DATA LOGGING & COMPREHENSIVE CSV SCHEMA
================================================================================
Every field inference event must append a row to a centralized CSV file 
(`field_trials_master_log.csv`) with the following column schema:

Column Headers:
  1.  Timestamp            (ISO-8601: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS)
  2.  Session_ID           (Unique session identifier, e.g., SESS_20261115_01)
  3.  Sample_Tag_ID        (Physical tree tag number, e.g., TAG_042)
  4.  Tree_Clone           (e.g., RRIM 600, PB 260)
  5.  Canopy_Level         (Lower / Middle / Upper)
  6.  Ground_Truth_Class   (Healthy / Other_Disease / PLFD)
  7.  Disease_Severity     (None / Mild / Moderate / Severe)
  8.  Lighting_Condition   (Midday_Sun / Canopy_Shade / Morning_Glare / Overcast)
  9.  Lux_Level_lx         (Numeric, e.g., 8450)
  10. Distance_meters      (Numeric, e.g., 1.0)
  11. Angle_degrees        (Numeric, e.g., 90, 45)
  12. Wind_Condition       (Still / Moderate / High)
  13. Camera_Sensor        (Pi_Camera_3_Wide / Arducam_16MP)
  14. GPS_Latitude         (Decimal degrees, e.g., 7.123456)
  15. GPS_Longitude        (Decimal degrees, e.g., 125.654321)
  16. GPS_Altitude_m       (Float, e.g., 142.5)
  17. GPS_Satellites       (Integer, e.g., 9)
  18. GPS_Fix_Status       (locked / searching / unavailable)
  19. GPS_Source           (Hardware_NEO-M8N / Mobile_Device_GPS)
  20. Stage1_Detected      (Boolean: True / False)
  21. Stage1_Confidence    (Float: 0.00 - 1.00)
  22. Stage1_BBox_Coords   (String: [x1, y1, x2, y2])
  23. Stage2_Pred_Class    (Healthy / Other_Disease / PLFD)
  24. Stage2_Confidence    (Float: 0.00 - 1.00)
  25. Prediction_Correct   (Boolean: True / False)
  26. Stage1_Latency_ms    (Float, e.g., 14.2)
  27. Stage2_Latency_ms    (Float, e.g., 8.7)
  28. Total_Latency_ms     (Float, e.g., 26.5)
  29. Measured_FPS         (Float, e.g., 37.7)
  30. Pi5_CPU_Temp_C       (Float, e.g., 54.3)
  31. Raw_Image_Path       (Relative filepath)
  32. Crop_Image_Path      (Relative filepath)

7. PHASE 6: POST-TRIAL DATA ANALYSIS & THESIS METRICS
================================================================================

7.1 Stage 1 Localization Evaluation:
  • Precision, Recall, mAP50, mAP50-95 computed against field-annotated bounding boxes.
  • Distance Degradation Curve: Plot Detection Rate (%) vs. Distance (0.5m to 4.0m).

7.2 Stage 2 Disease Classification Evaluation:
  • Multi-Class Confusion Matrix under real-world field conditions.
  • Macro-Averaged and Per-Class Metrics:
    - Precision = TP / (TP + FP)
    - Recall (Sensitivity) = TP / (TP + FN)
    - F1-Score = 2 * (Precision * Recall) / (Precision + Recall)
    - Specificity = TN / (TN + FP)

7.3 Environmental Factor Sensitivity Analysis:
  • Grouped Bar Plots: Accuracy and F1-Score categorized by:
    - Lighting condition (Midday Sun vs. Canopy Shade vs. Morning Glare vs. Overcast).
    - Distance intervals (<=1.0m vs. 1.5-2.0m vs. >=3.0m).
    - Optical Camera Sensor (Pi Camera 3 Wide vs. Arducam 16MP).
  • Statistical Significance Testing:
    - Perform One-Way Analysis of Variance (ANOVA) or Kruskal-Wallis test to verify 
      whether environmental variations cause statistically significant differences 
      in diagnostic accuracy (p < 0.05).

7.4 Geospatial Disease Epidemiology Analysis:
  • GIS Scatter & Heatmaps: Plot disease occurrences against GPS latitude/longitude.
  • Cluster Analysis: Identify high-density infection hotspots across plantation blocks.

7.5 Edge Hardware Profiling Results:
  • End-to-end latency distribution histogram (Preprocessing, Stage 1, Stage 2, Total).
  • Time-series thermal stability plot (Pi 5 CPU temperature over 2 hours of continuous load).
  • Mean Power Drain and estimated field battery autonomy (hours per 10,000mAh).

YEAR 2026: SYSTEM OPTIMIZATION, INTEGRATION & FIELD EVALUATION
================================================================================

[AUGUST 2026] - FOUNDATIONAL PREPARATION & NPU SETUP (COMPLETED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Week 1: Raw data curation, Roboflow dataset structuring, Stage 1 bounding box 
          annotations, Stage 2 disease cropping, 70/20/10 split validation.
• Week 2: Hardware acquisition (Raspberry Pi 5 8GB, active cooler, official casing,
          Camera Module 3 Wide, 27W 5V/5A power adapter, Hailo-8L AI HAT).
• Week 3: Cloud GPU model training (Stage 1 YOLO detector 100 epochs, Stage 2 
          multi-class disease classifier, confusion matrices, test-set metrics).
• Week 4: Raspberry Pi 5 hardware assembly, 64-bit OS flash, PCIe Gen 3 setup,
          Hailo driver suite installation, and ONNX-to-HEF compilation.

[SEPTEMBER 2026] - EDGE PIPELINE INTEGRATION & MULTI-MODEL BENCHMARKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Activity 1: Two-Stage Standalone Integration
  - Integrate Stage 1 (YOLO leaf detection) and Stage 2 (disease classification)
    into a unified inference pipeline on Raspberry Pi 5.
  - Implement dynamic ROI cropping and memory buffer sharing between stages.
• Activity 2: Hard Negative Mining & Background Noise Suppression
  - Introduce 100+ background-only images (soil, tree trunks, non-rubber weed)
    into Stage 1 training to suppress outdoor false positives.
  - Recalibrate operational confidence threshold (conf = 0.40 - 0.50).
• Activity 3: Cross-Generational Model Benchmark
  - Train and compare YOLO generations (YOLOv8, YOLOv9, YOLOv10, YOLO11) and 
    model sizes (Nano, Small, Medium) across mAP50, mAP50-95, GFLOPs, and params.
• Activity 4: Local Web Dashboard & Hardware GNSS Geotagging Finalization
  - Finalize lightweight Flask diagnostic dashboard for local Wi-Fi and mobile hotspot.
  - Implement and verify hardware GNSS / GPS module integration (u-blox NEO-M8N) 
    with asynchronous NMEA parsing for precise geospatial field logging.

[OCTOBER 2026] - EXPLAINABLE AI (XAI) VALIDATION & EDGE PROFILING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Activity 1: Explainability & Interpretability Analysis
  - Generate Grad-CAM, Grad-CAM++, and Eigen-CAM visual saliency heatmaps.
  - Conduct quantitative perturbation analysis: Most Relevant First (MoRF) 
    and Least Relevant First (LeRF) area-over-perturbation curve (AOPC).
• Activity 2: Hardware Edge Profiling Under Continuous Load
  - Profile end-to-end latency (ms), frame rate (FPS), CPU/RAM utilization,
    and thermal dissipation on Raspberry Pi 5 with Hailo-8L NPU.
  - Measure power consumption stability under 5V/5A power delivery.
• Activity 3: Standalone Research Paper Draft 1
  - Draft and finalize the research paper: "Beyond Accuracy: Explainability-Driven 
    Performance Analysis of Deep Learning Models for Rubber Leaf Disease Diagnosis".

[NOVEMBER 2026] - FIELD VALIDATION & ENVIRONMENTAL TESTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Activity 1: Plantation Field Trials (Mindanao Rubber Plantations)
  - Deploy handheld edge prototype in local rubber plantations.
  - Test real-time diagnostic performance on live trees across five disease
    categories (Powdery Mildew, Bird's Eye Spot, Colletotrichum, Pestalotiopsis).
• Activity 2: Environmental Stress Testing
  - Evaluate detection robustness across variable lighting conditions (direct 
    midday sunlight, canopy shade, overcast, morning glare).
  - Map distance degradation curves (testing operational ranges: 0.5m to 4.0m).
• Activity 3: Edge Usability Data Logging
  - Log field inference failure cases, motion blur sensitivity, and false-positive 
    triggers for iterative model refinement.

[DECEMBER 2026] - STANDARDIZED QUALITY & USABILITY EVALUATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Activity 1: ISO/IEC 25010 Software Quality Assessment
  - Administer structured ISO/IEC 25010 evaluation surveys with IT experts, 
    software engineers, and agronomists across 8 quality dimensions:
    (Functional Suitability, Performance Efficiency, Compatibility, Usability,
     Reliability, Security, Maintainability, Portability).
• Activity 2: Post-Study System Usability Questionnaire (PSSUQ)
  - Conduct user testing sessions with agricultural technicians and smallholder 
    rubber farmers; collect PSSUQ usability and user satisfaction scores.
• Activity 3: Data Consolidation & Statistical Synthesis
  - Compute Mean Opinion Scores (MOS), Cronbach's alpha reliability coefficients, 
    and usability sub-scale metrics (SysUse, InfoQual, IntQual).

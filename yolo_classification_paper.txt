Authors: Autonomous AI Research System & Deep Learning Group

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ABSTRACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Foliar leaf diseases in natural rubber (Hevea brasiliensis)—specifically 
Colletotrichum Leaf Spot and Pestalotiopsis Leaf Fall Disease—cause severe 
canopy defoliation and latex yield degradation, requiring rapid, edge-deployable 
automated diagnostic solutions. This study grounds its investigation in 
single-stage convolutional neural network architecture paradigms, leveraging 
You Only Look Once (YOLO) classification heads (-cls) for low-latency feature 
extraction and spatial representation on resource-constrained hardware. 
Comparing lightweight YOLO generations clarifies model selection trade-offs 
for precision agriculture, balancing top-1 accuracy against class-specific 
sensitivity for field deployment. Under a controlled 70:20:10 stratified split 
(N ~ 4,900 total images, 224x224 resolution, 100 epochs, Adam optimizer, 
batch size 32), we benchmarked YOLOv8n-cls, YOLO11n-cls, and YOLO26n-cls on 
490 held-out test specimens (Colletotrichum: 227, Healthy: 177, Pestalotiopsis: 86). 
Empirical results demonstrate that YOLOv8n-cls achieved the highest overall 
accuracy (92.45%) and weighted F1-score (92.39%). YOLO11n-cls yielded the highest 
macro precision (91.22%), macro F1-score (90.40%), and top Pestalotiopsis 
F1-score (81.71%). YOLO26n-cls achieved perfect recall (100.00%) on healthy 
foliage but lower sensitivity on Pestalotiopsis (68.60% recall). Consequently, 
YOLOv8n-cls is recommended for general field screening, while YOLO11n-cls is 
optimal for early-stage Pestalotiopsis identification on edge mobile nodes.

Keywords: Precision Agriculture, Rubber Leaf Pathology, YOLO Classification, 
Colletotrichum, Pestalotiopsis, Edge AI Deployment, Machine Learning Performance.

4. CLASS-WISE CONFUSION MATRIX & ERROR DISTRIBUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Healthy Leaf Discrimination:
  YOLO26n-cls achieved 100.00% Recall (177/177 correctly identified, 0 false negatives).
  YOLOv8n-cls achieved 99.44% Recall (176/177 correctly identified, 1 false negative).
  YOLO11n-cls achieved 98.31% Recall (174/177 correctly identified, 3 false negatives).

- Colletotrichum Spot Identification:
  YOLO11n-cls and YOLO26n-cls both achieved 92.95% Recall (211/227 correctly identified).
  YOLOv8n-cls achieved 92.51% Recall (210/227 correctly identified).

- Pestalotiopsis (PLFD) Sensitivity:
  YOLOv8n-cls and YOLO11n-cls both achieved 77.91% Recall (67/86 correctly identified).
  YOLO26n-cls achieved 68.60% Recall (59/86 correctly identified).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CONCLUSION & PRACTICAL DEPLOYMENT RECOMMENDATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. General Deployment Recommendation:
   Deploy YOLOv8n-cls for general field screening. It delivers the highest 
   overall test accuracy (92.45%) and weighted F1-score (92.39%).

2. Early Pathogen Detection Recommendation:
   Deploy YOLO11n-cls when identifying Pestalotiopsis Leaf Fall Disease. It 
   achieves the highest macro F1-score (90.40%) and the top Pestalotiopsis 
   F1-score (81.71%).

3. Zero False Alarm Recommendation:
   Deploy YOLO26n-cls when zero false alarms on healthy foliage are strictly 
   required (100.00% Healthy recall), accepting reduced recall on minor lesions.

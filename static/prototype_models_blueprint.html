Master's Thesis Engineering Blueprint

Two-Stage Edge AI Diagnostic Hardware Prototypes

Three physical prototype designs engineered for Raspberry Pi 5 + Hailo-8L AI HAT+ (13 TOPS) + Sony IMX708 PDAF + u-blox NEO-M8N GNSS for Rubber Leaf Disease detection (PLFD).

Model 1: Handheld Unit

AgroWand-5 (Integrated Scanner)

All-in-one ergonomic pistol-grip diagnostic device. Ideal for nurseries, lower-canopy surveying, and seedling disease staging.

  • Operating Mode: One-handed Point & Shoot
  • Weight: ~620 grams (with battery)
  • Display: 5.0" DSI Touchscreen (Rear)
  • Power Source: Internal 10,000 mAh PD Grip
  • Optics: IMX708 PDAF (±45° Swivel)
  • Enclosure: Unibody PETG / ASA (IP54)
Fast Sampling Hardware Shutter Zero Snag Risk
Model 2: Canopy Extension

CanopyReach (Telescopic Rig)

Modular 4-meter telescopic carbon-fiber pole system for sampling high crowns where Pestalotiopsis initiates defoliation.

  • Operating Mode: High-Canopy Reaching (up to 4.5m)
  • Weight (Head): < 95 grams (no bend)
  • Display: 7.0" Screen at Chest Height
  • Counterweight: Battery pack mounted at base
  • GPS Reception: Highest (elevated above brush)
  • Materials: Carbon Fiber Tube + 3D Mounts
Mature Crowns Ladder-Free Superior GPS
Model 3: Split Wearable

FieldPack-Edge (Tethered Kit)

Split-architecture system isolating compute & battery into a hip/chest pack. Eliminates arm fatigue during all-day field surveys.

  • Operating Mode: Continuous plantation walk
  • In-Hand Wand: ~190 grams (Ultra-lightweight)
  • Display: 3.5" OLED on wand handle
  • Compute & Battery: Worn on waist holster
  • Battery Runtime: 6.0 - 8.0+ continuous hours
  • Thermal Headroom: Optimal (open pack ventilation)
Zero Fatigue All-Day Power Maximum Cooling
